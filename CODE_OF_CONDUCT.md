# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Tube Arena should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Treat people with dignity regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be considerate and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and corrections.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Maintaining the standard

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately through GitHub to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed as fairly as possible.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.
